Kakitori privacy policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Kakitori doesn't collect, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the developer never receives your data.

What stays on your device

Your practice progress and settings (language, theme, review schedule and reminder time) are saved in the app's own storage on your phone, or in your browser for the web version. Deleting the app, clearing its data, or using Start over in Settings erases them.

On Android, if you have turned on device backup in your Google account, Android may include this data in that backup. That backup is run by Google under your account settings.

Optional sync

Sync is off unless you turn it on. When you do, your progress goes straight from your device to an account you own. It never passes through a server run by the developer.

  • GitHub: progress is saved to a private repository in your GitHub account, using a token you create. The token is stored only on your device. GitHub's privacy statement applies to data stored there.
  • Google: progress is saved through an Apps Script you set up in your Google account. Anyone who knows your script's address can read and change your progress, so keep it private. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Backup codes: a backup code is text you copy yourself. Kakitori doesn't send it anywhere.
  • Inside Claude: when you use Kakitori as an artifact in Claude, progress is saved to your Claude account, under Anthropic's privacy policy.

Reminders

On Android, the daily practice reminder is scheduled on your phone. No server is involved. You can turn reminders off in Kakitori's settings or in your phone's notification settings at any time.

Internet use

The Android app works offline. It only connects to the internet to sync (when you have turned sync on) and when you open a link, such as a setup page.

The iPhone web version is downloaded from GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when the page loads. After the first visit it also works offline, and it doesn't load anything from other sites.

Android permissions

  • Internet: for sync and opening links.
  • Notifications: for the daily reminder, which stays off until you turn it on.
  • Run at startup: to set the reminder again after your phone restarts.

Children

Kakitori doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can go to the developer email address shown on Kakitori's Google Play listing, or to the project's issue page on GitHub.
